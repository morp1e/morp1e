## About

I'm a Digital Game Design student based in Northern Cyprus, learning how to orchestrate AI systems and turn them into useful creative workflows. My work sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, interactive design, digital art, and personal knowledge systems.

I'm currently in the learning-and-building phase: testing ideas, documenting what works, and turning small experiments into credible portfolio projects. This profile is a record of that process—not a claim that I have everything figured out.

> **Solve et coagula** — take systems apart, understand their parts, and recombine them with intent.

## Current focus

- **AI operator workflows** — connecting models, tools, memory, and human judgment
- **Creative systems** — exploring AI as a medium for game design and digital art
- **Knowledge infrastructure** — building better ways to capture, connect, and apply what I learn
- **Proof of work** — turning experiments into clear documentation, reusable workflows, and finished projects
